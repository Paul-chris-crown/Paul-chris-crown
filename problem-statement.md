# Problem Statement

The gap between real-world asset ownership and on-chain representation remains largely underutilized due to:

&#x20;• Complex regulatory and verification processes

&#x20;• Illiquid real-world asset markets

&#x20;• Limitations in existing NFT standards

&#x20;• Lack of platforms supporting both fungibility and non-fungibility in a hybrid format

\


RAXUS addresses these challenges by providing a dual-layered NFT marketplace that brings real-world value to Web3 ecosystems.
