# Use Cases

Use Case Description

* Real Estate Tokenization Fractional ownership of physical property traded as f-NFTs
* Digital Art Trading Artists mint and sell 1/1 digital or phygital art as NFTs
* Physical Asset Markets Precious metals, watches, and rare items stored off-chain, sold on-chain
* Community Co-ownership Collectives pool funds to own high-value assets transparently
